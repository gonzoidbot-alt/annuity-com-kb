- **Objection handling steps** — Thank them; empathize/acknowledge ('before I got in the business I said the same'); ask 'May I ask why?' to flush false objections; show value to them and family; use disinterested third-party references.
- **Know your product cold** — Know what's guaranteed vs not, caps, participation rates, whether guaranteed.
- **Pre-empt objections in the presentation** — Put Ken Fisher/fees objections in slides; or ignore ('I understand') and continue; or defer ('coming in three slides').
- **Stop selling; be a problem finder and solver** — Advice to his daughter Sammy: 'Stop selling.'
- **Fees objection: CD/bank analogy** — Bank pays 4%, lends at 9%, keeps spread; annuities mostly spread products.
- **Commission objection** — Ask 'How much of the commission do you pay?' – zero; contrast with advisory fees that always come from the account. Brett: disclose compensation upfront (John Stevenson shows commissions on multiple products).
- **FDIC objection** — Ask if FDIC matters; FDIC can't cover major banks; buy CDs from smallest weakest banks or choose strong insurer (humorous).
- **Liquidity objection** — Liquidity is a lifetime event; annuity increases lifetime liquidity; MYGAs usually allow 10% penalty-free; many CDs allow none.
- **'Market returns 15% vs 8%' objection** — Apples to oranges; for market exposure use VA or RILA; emphasize downside security, not rate of return.
- **100% in stocks** — Show sequence of returns risk with software (Wealth Logics); 'never take money out of an account that's going down'; plant a seed with 100%-stock holders and invite a later call.
- **Risk tolerance questionnaires** — Not very helpful; ask 'How would you feel if $1M became $600K next week?'
- **Inflation objection** — Ladder multiple annuities starting at different times, add COLA, keep some money in market.
- **CD season outreach / triple interest** — Talk to CD holders in fall; show MYGA, then blend MYGA with FIA so they never get zero.
- **Social Security tax torpedo as lead gen** — 'Easiest conversation – you're not selling anything'; move CDs/tax-free bonds into annuities to get clients from 85% taxed to 0%; leads to word-of-mouth referrals.
- **Don't prejudge** — Manufactured-housing neighborhoods often have clients with $400–500K in CDs who want guarantees; high-end homes may be indebted.
- **Beat advisors with math/AI side-by-side** — Run advisor's recommendation vs yours through software/AI; show annuity-in-portfolio wins in distribution.
- **Not every call ends in a sale** — Brett's police-officer story: told retiree 'you don't need me' – role is worry-free understanding.
